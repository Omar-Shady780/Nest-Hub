Core Essential Features

Designed from the ground up for tablets on bedside docks, stands, and charging tables with zero compromises.

🌙

OLED Pure Black StandBy Clocks

Fullscreen ambient nightstand clocks designed for deep sleep. Clocks smoothly fade into pure zero-backlight OLED black without lighting up your room.

  • Digital Clock with live seconds right beside hours and minutes
  • Minimal Analog Chronograph watchface with smooth sweeping hands
  • Live Next Scheduled Alarm & USB / Battery status pills
  • Weather forecast with Celsius (°C) and Fahrenheit (°F) support
⊞

Custom Grid & Dynamic Slot Sizing

Build your ideal dashboard grid layout with configurable dimensions and flexible card sizing.

  • Choose between 2×2 Grid (4 large focus widgets) or 3×3 Grid (9 widgets)
  • Custom Grid Layout: Configurable rows (2-4), columns (2-4), and dynamic add/remove slot buttons
  • Per-widget card spans: Normal Width (1x), Wide Width (2x), or Tall Height (2x)
  • Pinch or long-press on any card for instant zoom customization dialogs
📱

Android Home Screen Widget Host

Bring the power of your favorite Android apps directly onto your bedside dashboard.

  • Native AppWidgetHost embeds Spotify, Google Calendar, Google Keep, Notes, or WhatsApp
  • Blends seamlessly with the card background colors and rounded corners
  • Full interactive touch support for system widget controls
  • Adapts dynamically to wallpaper colors and themes
☀️

Smart Physical Volume Key Controls

Smart dual-mode hardware button routing designed specifically for bedside tables.

  • Press physical volume keys to smoothly dim or brighten screen brightness
  • Sleek Sun percentage HUD popup appears without covering your view
  • Automatically routes to media volume whenever music or audio plays
  • Zero-sleep wake lock keeps the screen alive docked without turning off
